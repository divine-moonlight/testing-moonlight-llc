WHAT WE PROVIDE

Our services

Our services are designed to support comfort, safety, independence, and quality of life at home.

01
EVERYDAY SENIOR SUPPORT

Senior Care

Personalized in-home support for older adults who want to remain comfortable and independent in familiar surroundings.

  • Daily routines and activities
  • Companionship and conversation
  • Household and organization support
  • Care tailored to the individual
02
MEMORY SUPPORT

Dementia & Alzheimer’s Support

Patient, consistent support for individuals living with dementia or Alzheimer’s, with an emphasis on dignity and familiar routines.

  • Companionship and reassurance
  • Familiar daily routines
  • Everyday activity support
  • Family communication as appropriate
03
MOVEMENT & DAILY LIVING

Parkinson’s Support

Thoughtful assistance for people living with Parkinson’s, focused on comfort, independence, safe routines, and everyday activities.

  • Daily routine assistance
  • Safe movement support
  • Companionship and encouragement
  • Flexible support as needs change
05
PERSONAL SUPPORT

Personal Care

Respectful assistance with personal routines and activities of daily living, provided with privacy and dignity.

  • Bathing and grooming routines
  • Dressing and personal organization
  • Mobility and routine assistance
  • Individualized care-plan support
06
RECOVERY SUPPORT

Surgery Recovery

Short-term in-home assistance for clients recovering after surgery, helping with everyday needs while they regain strength and independence.

  • Personal routine and mobility support
  • Meal preparation and light housekeeping
  • Transportation and appointment support
  • Companionship during recovery
07
CONNECTION & COMPANIONSHIP

Companionship

Friendly social support that helps clients stay connected, engaged, and less isolated at home.

  • Conversation and shared activities
  • Reading, games, and hobbies
  • Accompaniment around the home
  • Encouragement to stay engaged
08
FAMILY SUPPORT

Respite Care

Temporary support that gives family caregivers time to work, rest, run errands, or take a needed break.

  • Short-term scheduled support
  • Familiar routines
  • Companionship and everyday assistance
  • Flexible family support
09
HOME SUPPORT

Light Housekeeping

Everyday household help that keeps the home comfortable, organized, and easier to manage.

  • Light cleaning and tidying
  • Laundry and linen changes
  • Common-area organization
  • Household support related to care
10
NOURISHMENT

Meal Preparation

Thoughtful meal support based on individual preferences and everyday routines.

  • Meal planning
  • Everyday meals and snacks
  • Kitchen cleanup
  • Grocery-related planning
11
EVERYDAY TASKS

Errands & Household Support

Practical help with tasks that can become difficult to manage alone.

  • Essential errands and shopping
  • Everyday household items
  • Basic organization and planning
  • Other appropriate care-plan tasks
12
TRANSPORTATION

Medical Appointment Transportation

Transportation support for eligible medical appointments and essential outings.

  • Transportation to and from appointments
  • Support before leaving and after returning
  • Appointment coordination with family
  • Transportation arrangements in advance
