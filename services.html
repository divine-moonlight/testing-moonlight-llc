PERSONALIZED SUPPORT

More than help around the house.

Every family has a different situation. Some need a little help with daily routines. Others need more consistent support, specialized care, or nursing services.

We work with families to understand what is needed and create support that fits into everyday life. Services may be combined and adjusted as needs change.

WHAT WE PROVIDE

Our services

Our services are designed to support comfort, safety, independence, and quality of life at home.

01
EVERYDAY SENIOR SUPPORT

Senior Care

Personalized in-home support for older adults who want to remain comfortable and independent in familiar surroundings.

  • Help with everyday routines and activities
  • Companionship and conversation
  • Support with household tasks and daily organization
  • Care tailored to the individual and their family
02
MEMORY SUPPORT

Dementia & Alzheimer’s Support

Support for individuals living with dementia or Alzheimer’s, with an emphasis on patience, familiarity, dignity, and a calm daily routine.

  • Companionship and reassuring presence
  • Help maintaining familiar routines
  • Support with everyday activities
  • Family communication and updates as appropriate
03
MOVEMENT & DAILY LIVING

Parkinson’s Support

Thoughtful support for people living with Parkinson’s, focused on maintaining routines, comfort, independence, and participation in everyday life.

  • Assistance with daily routines
  • Support with safe movement and household activities
  • Companionship and encouragement
  • Flexible support as needs change
04
✦
SAFETY AT HOME

Fall Prevention

Practical support to help reduce common fall risks at home and make everyday movement safer and more comfortable.

  • Identify common household trip and fall hazards
  • Keep walkways and frequently used areas clear
  • Support safer routines around the home
  • Encourage use of appropriate mobility and safety equipment already recommended by a healthcare professional

Fall prevention support is not a substitute for a medical evaluation or physical therapy. We can work alongside the recommendations of your healthcare team.

05
PERSONAL SUPPORT

Personal Care

Respectful assistance with personal routines and activities of daily living, provided with privacy and dignity.

  • Support with bathing and grooming routines
  • Dressing and personal organization
  • Mobility and routine assistance
  • Individualized support based on the care plan
06
CONNECTION & COMPANIONSHIP

Companionship

Sometimes the most important part of care is simply having someone there. Companionship helps make the day feel less isolated and more connected.

  • Conversation, reading, games, and shared activities
  • Accompaniment around the home
  • Encouragement to stay engaged with everyday interests
  • Friendly social support for individuals who spend significant time at home
07
FAMILY SUPPORT

Respite Care

Reliable temporary support that gives family caregivers time to work, rest, run errands, or simply take a break while their loved one remains cared for.

  • Short-term and scheduled support
  • Help maintaining familiar routines
  • Companionship and everyday assistance
  • Flexible support based on family needs and availability
08
HOME SUPPORT

Light Housekeeping

Help with everyday household tasks that keep the home comfortable, organized, and easier to manage.

  • Light cleaning and tidying
  • Laundry and linen changes
  • Keeping commonly used areas organized
  • Everyday household support related to the client’s care
09
NOURISHMENT

Meal Preparation

Simple, thoughtful meal support that helps clients maintain regular meals while keeping their preferences and routines in mind.

  • Meal planning around individual preferences
  • Preparing everyday meals and snacks
  • Kitchen cleanup after meal preparation
  • Support with grocery-related planning when included in the care plan
10
EVERYDAY TASKS

Errands & Household Support

Help with practical tasks that can become difficult to manage alone, allowing clients and families to spend more time on what matters.

  • Essential errands and shopping support
  • Picking up everyday household items
  • Basic organization and planning
  • Other appropriate tasks discussed as part of the care plan
11
GETTING WHERE YOU NEED TO GO

Medical Appointment Transportation

Dependable transportation support for eligible appointments and essential outings, helping clients get to and from important destinations.

  • Transportation to and from medical appointments
  • Support getting ready to leave and return home
  • Assistance coordinating appointment details with the family
  • Transportation arrangements discussed before service begins
12
CLINICAL SUPPORT

Nursing Pool

Access to nursing and care professionals for families and organizations looking for additional support, including RN, LPN, and CNA staffing.

  • Registered Nurse (RN) support
  • Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) support
  • Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA) support
  • Flexible staffing needs based on availability and appropriate scope of practice
13
✦
FAMILY COMMUNICATION

Care Coordination & Family Support

We help families stay organized and informed as they navigate care at home. The goal is to make the process easier to understand and easier to manage.

  • Discussing changing needs and routines
  • Communication with designated family members
  • Helping families keep track of service needs and schedules
  • Working alongside the client’s existing healthcare team when appropriate and authorized
A CARE PLAN THAT FITS

Services can work together.

A client may need more than one type of support. For example, a family might combine personal care, meal preparation, fall-prevention support, transportation, and companionship.

We can talk through what is needed and determine which services make sense for your situation.
